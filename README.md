A kind, funny fortune for the houseplant on the windowsill: the sun leans in to gossip with it, and its keeper brags about it to everyone. `main.py` writes the fortune to `entry.txt`.

> The sun will lean through the window to gossip with you, little leaf — your keeper will brag about you to everyone.

Contract: one line, at most 120 characters, UTF-8 text.
